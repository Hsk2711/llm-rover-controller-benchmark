Column definitions for results/llm_generation_log.csv (kept here because a CSV header row cannot carry comments without breaking readers)
- model, model_version, date, attempt (valid attempts 1-5 per chatbot; X1 = excluded attempt, not a temporary chat), 
  one_shot_compiled, feedback_rounds (max 3, compile-error rounds count), final_compiled, final_quicktest_score (of the LAST round used),
  notes.
- human_minutes = active human time per attempt, from opening the chatbot chat to the final code being saved,
  EXCLUDING time waiting for compile and Quick Test. Values entered after the fact are marked "estimated afterwards" in notes.
